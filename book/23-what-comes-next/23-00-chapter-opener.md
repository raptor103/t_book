# 23. What comes next

This is not a chapter of predictions — the graveyard of confident technology forecasts is vast. It is a short chapter about *where the frontiers are*: the places where the hardest problems remain unsolved, and where the next real gains will have to come from if they come at all.

Three frontiers matter most. The first is the **battery** — still the component with the most room left to improve, as it has been since before the electric car could even leave the wings. The second is **autonomy** — the car that drives itself, which is the boldest thing the industry, and Tesla above all, has promised, and the one still furthest from delivered. The third is **efficiency** itself, the organising obsession of Chapter 1, now so nearly perfected inside the drivetrain that the honest question is how much is really left to win.

The sections that follow take those frontiers briefly in turn, and then the book ends — not with a grand prophecy, but by returning to the driveway where it began.
